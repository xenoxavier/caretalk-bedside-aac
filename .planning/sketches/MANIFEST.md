# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
CareTalk is a bedside AAC (augmentative/alternative communication) touchscreen app for a bedridden, non-speaking patient with limited dexterity, used bilingually (English/German). The direction is calm, high-contrast, and pictogram-led rather than clinical-sterile: a warm paper ground, ink-dark text, Atkinson Hyperlegible for every patient-facing label (a typeface literally designed for low-vision legibility), and quick-request buttons color-coded by category in the style of the Fitzgerald key used on real AAC devices. Semantic color is reserved for meaning — teal for confirm/SPEAK, red for PAIN/alert — never used as decoration. Large touch targets throughout (96–110px), minimal motion, and every screen keeps PAIN and SPEAK reachable in one tap.

## Reference Points
Real AAC devices (Proloquo2Go, TouchChat) and their Fitzgerald-key color coding; hospital nurse-call bells (used as the Help icon); bedside vital-sign monitors (tabular-numeral styling for the pain-severity scale); the source plan's own emphasis on one-tap access, high contrast, and no unnecessary animation.
